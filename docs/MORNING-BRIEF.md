## How to write the brief
- Keep it **tight and skimmable** — it goes to his phone. Concrete next actions, not vague ("Call 5 HVAC
  companies from the list" beats "do outreach").
- Pull the **real current state** each morning (knowledge/, docs/DEPLOYS.md, scheduled reminders) — skip
  anything already handled.
- Be **opinionated + motivating** without padding. If the top priority is obvious (get a client), say it plainly.
- **Read-only:** never commit, deploy, email, or change anything — just read and summarize.
