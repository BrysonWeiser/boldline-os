### 💪 Gym / health
**Split:** Mon **Push** · Tue **Pull** · Wed **Legs** · Thu **Arms** · Fri **Plyometrics (vertical)** · Sat/Sun flex or rest.
(Wed Legs → Fri Plyos = 2 days recovery, intentional — plyos need fresh legs.) The brief should name **today's** session.

**Friday — Plyometrics / Vertical (~45 min, do FRESH, quality > quantity, 90s–2min full rest between sets, land soft):**
- Warm-up (5–8 min): jump rope 2 min → leg swings → walking lunges → 2×10 ankle pogo hops → 5 sub-max squat jumps
- 1. Box jumps 4×4 (step down, never jump down)
- 2. Depth jumps 3×4 (12–18" box; step off, land, immediately explode up — add only once box jumps feel easy)
- 3. Broad jumps 3×4
- 4. Approach max vertical jumps 4×3 (practice the real jump-and-reach)
- 5. Single-leg bounds 3×5 each leg
- 6. Tuck jumps 3×6
- 7. Ankle pogo hops 3×12 (stiff legs, all ankle)
- Finish: stretch calves, quads, hips.
- ⚠️ Never grind plyos when sore/tired (CNS-heavy, injury risk). Heavy squats/deadlifts on Leg day = ~50% of vertical gains — keep pushing those. Progress by slowly adding height/reps. 1–2×/week max.

## How to write the brief
- **Adapt to the day of week:** weekday = gym + cold calls + BoldLine + day-job reality (morning only). Weekend = build/reselling + recovery, NO cold calls.
- Name **today's specific gym session** from the split (and on Fridays, remind the plyo focus, not the full list).
- Keep it **tight, skimmable, motivating** — it goes to his phone. Concrete next actions ("Call 25 HVAC shops from your list, book 1 meeting" beats "do outreach").
- Pull the **real current state** each morning (knowledge/, docs/DEPLOYS.md) — skip anything already handled.
- Be **opinionated.** If the top priority is obvious (get a client), say it plainly.
- **Read-only:** never commit, deploy, email, or change anything — just read and summarize.
