Founder video, take 2

Talking points, not lines. 30 to 45 seconds total.
Before you hit record
  • Dark shirt. Clean background: plain wall, desk with the OS, or outside.
  • HDR off: Settings › Camera › Record Video › HDR Video.
  • Stand up. Say it all out loud twice first.
While recording
  • Slow down. Pause after a big point.
  • Lean on one word per sentence.
  • Smile at the start and the end.
  • Talk to one business owner, not everyone.
  • 2 or 3 takes per point. 2 seconds of quiet between.
