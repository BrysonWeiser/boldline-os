# Founder video: "How I built a business that runs almost by itself"

Bryson records all of it in one take, talking to the camera. Claude cuts it, covers the system parts with real OS
screens (made-up sample businesses only, never a real client) and adds ARIA in her own voice.
About 80 seconds.

## The script (what Bryson says), v2 2026-10-21
Bryson: "I definitely want to say it's automated but there should be a reason ... so it takes less time to deliver
what the client paid for and so it gets rid of human error and also so that reports are 100% accurate and there is
never a miscommunication or lack of communication between me and my clients". "100% accurate" was advised against
(a late platform number or a disputed lead pokes a hole in it) and said as "the numbers come straight from Google and
Meta, nothing typed in by hand" instead. About 90 seconds.

[ARIA opens the video. Bryson says nothing here.]
ARIA: "Good morning, Bryson. Everything's ready when you are."

1. I'm Bryson. I run BoldLine Media, a marketing agency, and I automated almost all of it.
2. Not to cut corners. I did it so my clients get what they paid for faster, without the mistakes that come from
   doing everything by hand.
3. My job is making calls and closing deals. Everything else, the system handles.
4. It starts with finding businesses to call. I pick an industry and a city, and it finds real businesses and tells me
   which ones are worth calling first.
5. Before I even get on a call, it can build that business a full website. So instead of explaining what I'd do, I
   just show them.
6. When they say yes, the contract goes out from the system, and billing runs itself from there.
7. Then it builds their ads on Google and Meta, plus the landing pages those ads send people to. I just approve them.
8. Every lead comes into one place, and the reports pull the numbers straight from Google and Meta. Nothing gets typed
   in by hand, so nothing gets mixed up.
9. My clients can check their results anytime in their own portal, and they get updates without having to ask. So
   they always know where things stand.
10. And then there's ARIA.

[ARIA: "Hi, I'm ARIA. I work alongside Bryson every day, so nothing slips through."]

11. Hey ARIA, start Power Hour.

[ARIA: "Power Hour is live. First call is up. Let's go."]

12. She plans my day and runs my calling sessions, and I just talk to her.
13. That's the whole point. My clients get results faster and they're never left wondering what's going on, and my
    time goes into talking to business owners, not busywork.
14. If you want a system like this working for your business, go to boldlinemedia.com.

## How to record it
- Phone upright (vertical), at eye level, facing a window for light, in a quiet room.
- Look at the lens and say it like you're telling a friend, not reading.
- Leave about two seconds of quiet between each numbered line. If you trip on a line, pause, then start that line
  again. The mistakes get cut.
- Line 9 ("Hey ARIA, start Power Hour") goes to the camera too. Her answer is added in the edit.
- Send the whole clip when done (split it if it's too big to send).

## What covers each line (Claude's edit plan)

## Honesty notes (do not loosen)
- Only systems that are live today. Texting new leads back is built but switched off (Twilio), so it is not in here.
- "Almost all of it runs by itself" and "I just approve them" are deliberate: he sends contracts and approves builds.
- No client counts, no results, no real client names or data on screen.
- Clients do not get ARIA; the closing line offers "a system like this" (ads, landing pages, leads, portal), which
  clients do get.
