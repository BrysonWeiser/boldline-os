# Founder video: "How I built a business that runs almost by itself"

Bryson records all of it in one take, talking to the camera. Claude cuts it, covers the system parts with real OS
screens (made-up sample businesses only, never a real client) and adds ARIA in her own voice.
About 80 seconds.

## The script (what Bryson says)

[ARIA opens the video. Bryson says nothing here.]
ARIA: "Good morning, Bryson. Everything's ready when you are."

1. I'm Bryson. I run BoldLine Media, a marketing agency, and I built it so almost all of it runs by itself.
2. My job is making calls and closing deals. Everything else, the system handles.
3. It starts with finding businesses to call. I pick an industry and a city, and it finds real businesses and tells me
   which ones are worth calling first.
4. Before I even get on a call, it can build that business a full website. So instead of explaining what I'd do, I
   just show them.
5. When they say yes, the contract goes out from the system, and billing runs itself from there.
6. Then it builds their ads on Google and Meta, plus the landing pages those ads send people to. I just approve them.
7. Every lead comes into one place, and my clients can check their results anytime in their own portal.
8. And then there's ARIA.

[ARIA: "Hi, I'm ARIA. I work alongside Bryson every day, so nothing slips through."]

9. Hey ARIA, start Power Hour.

[ARIA: "Power Hour is live. First call is up. Let's go."]

10. She plans my day and runs my calling sessions, and I just talk to her.
11. That's the whole point. My time goes into talking to business owners and taking care of clients, not busywork.
12. If you want a system like this working for your business, go to boldlinemedia.com.

## How to record it
- Phone upright (vertical), at eye level, facing a window for light, in a quiet room.
- Look at the lens and say it like you're telling a friend, not reading.
- Leave about two seconds of quiet between each numbered line. If you trip on a line, pause, then start that line
  again. The mistakes get cut.
- Line 9 ("Hey ARIA, start Power Hour") goes to the camera too. Her answer is added in the edit.
- Send the whole clip when done (split it if it's too big to send).

## What covers each line (Claude's edit plan)
- ARIA opening: the OS opening on the Today screen with her glowing core, her greeting.
- 1, 2: Bryson on camera.
- 3: Lead Scout finding and scoring sample businesses.
- 4: a demo website for a sample business, "Preview for ..." strip and all.
- 5: an agreement and the automatic billing steps.
- 6: a campaign and the landing page it points to.
- 7: leads landing in the OS, then the client portal's weekly chart.
- 8: Bryson on camera, then ARIA's core as she introduces herself.
- 9: Bryson on camera, then the Outreach screen as Power Hour starts and she answers.
- 10: the Today screen and the Power Hour clock.
- 11, 12: Bryson on camera, end card with boldlinemedia.com.

## Honesty notes (do not loosen)
- Only systems that are live today. Texting new leads back is built but switched off (Twilio), so it is not in here.
- "Almost all of it runs by itself" and "I just approve them" are deliberate: he sends contracts and approves builds.
- No client counts, no results, no real client names or data on screen.
- Clients do not get ARIA; the closing line offers "a system like this" (ads, landing pages, leads, portal), which
  clients do get.
