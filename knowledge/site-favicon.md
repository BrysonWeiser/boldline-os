**Why (Bryson, 2026-07-29):** the logo looked white/washed-out in the Google search result for boldlinemedia.com. Root cause: `marketing-site/logo.png` is a gold "BL" monogram on a **transparent** background, so on Google's white card (and light browser tabs) there's effectively no logo visible.

**The fix — a black-background icon, as TWO files at the marketing-site root:**
- **`marketing-site/icon.png`** — 512×512, the gold monogram centered (~62% of canvas, generated by trimming logo.png to its `getbbox()` then compositing on a **rounded** near-black `#0D0D0D` square). Used for `apple-touch-icon` + the high-res `rel=icon`.
- **`marketing-site/favicon.ico`** — multi-size ICO (16/32/48/64), gold monogram on a **solid** (un-rounded) black square with slightly less padding so it's legible at 16px. This is the universal fallback path (`/favicon.ico`) that Google + browsers check first — it was **404ing** before this, which is worth fixing regardless of the cache issue.
- Both generated with Python **Pillow** (`pip install Pillow`); the source is always `logo.png` cropped to its bounding box.

**Head declarations (all 5 marketing pages — index/get-started/privacy/terms/404 — AND blog pages via `marketing-site/netlify/lib/blog-render.mjs`):**
```
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" href="/icon.png">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/icon.png">
```

**Do NOT touch the in-page logo:** the nav `<img src="/logo.png">` stays the **transparent** logo.png — it sits on the dark header where transparent gold is correct. Baking black into logo.png would put a black box in the header. Favicon (search/tab) and in-page logo are deliberately different files.

**THE GOTCHA — Google favicon lag (this is the #1 thing to remember):** after deploying, `curl https://boldlinemedia.com/icon.png` and the homepage `<link rel=icon>` confirmed the black icon is **live and correct**, but the **Google search result still showed the old white/transparent one**. Google caches favicons and only refreshes them when it re-crawls the homepage — **days to weeks**. This is expected, not a bug. Proof it's working: open `/icon.png` directly or check the browser tab (after a hard refresh) — both show black. To *nudge* it faster you'd request re-indexing in Google Search Console (not set up yet). A reminder was set (2026-07-30) to re-check the search result.
