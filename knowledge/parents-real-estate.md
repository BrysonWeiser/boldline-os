Q (Bryson, 2026-11-17): his parents are real estate agents who aren't pushing it; mom says she would with leads.
Worth building them a site + running ads free for a month or two?

Answer given: YES, but as a structured practice run, not a favour.
- Worth it for: a low-stakes full run of the system (site, housing-category ads already built per KB housing-ads,
  lead follow-up), real numbers he can quote, and family who will tell him what is broken.
- Not worth it as: a testimonial (family is weak proof) or a niche move (real estate is heavily farmed; he targets
  detailers and service trades, KB niche-selection).
- Conditions: (1) they pay ad spend on their own card and their own account (hard constraint); only his management
  is free. Real estate leads cost roughly $20-60+ each on Google, so budget ~$1,000/mo or don't bother. (2) 90 days,
  not 30-60: online real estate leads close in months, so judge by conversations and appointments, not sales.
  (3) Mom calls every lead within 5 minutes and follows up for weeks; most online leads are cold. Biggest risk is
  leads arrive, aren't worked, and the verdict is "ads don't work". (4) Seller leads (free home value page) over buyer
  leads. (5) Brokerage rules first: license number, brokerage name, Equal Housing logo, ad approval; check whether
  the brokerage already gives them a site. (6) Cap his hours so it doesn't eat cold-calling time. (7) Run it through the
  OS like a real client (a free agreement) for practice, but do NOT count them for the founding offer / client count.
- Real estate website pieces (listings, home valuation form, compliance footer) are still NOT built (KB housing-ads).
