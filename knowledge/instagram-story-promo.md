**His asks, in order (2026-09-24, evening Phoenix):** a story image for "taking on a few service
based businesses (renovation, car detailers, etc.)" and "DM me if interested"; then "also include I
do meta as well"; "don't add any buttons that won't work"; "include how they only pay for real
results not tire kickers"; replace the free-website-look line; then *"look at it as if you were some
random business ... would you dm me"*; then remake it plus a video script.

## What shipped (v2)

1080x1920 PNG, BoldLine ink/gold, all content inside Instagram's safe zone (top ~250px and bottom
~300px are covered by the app). Rendered by a throwaway Playwright script in the session scratchpad,
not kept in the repo: rebuild from this description if needed.

- Kicker: **For service businesses**
- Headline: **Pay for / real leads. / Not tire kickers.**
- Line: I run your **Google, Facebook and Instagram ads** and build the **page they land on**.
- Niche strip: Remodeling · Detailing · Pools · Cleaning
- Poll question printed on the image (added v3): **"Own a service business and have room for more jobs?"**, with an empty band under it **on purpose, for Instagram's Yes/No poll sticker**. No fake poll is drawn, because a drawn button that does nothing breaks his rule.
  🔴 **Why that question:** it screens out the two people he cannot help (not an owner; an owner already turning work away) and costs one tap. Money qualifiers (budget, job value) are deliberately left for the DM, because a price question in a story poll makes the people who would say yes skip it.
- CTA: **DM me "LEADS"** + "I'll tell you straight whether ads would pay off for you."

## The decisions, and why

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| "You pay per real lead", not "only pay for results" | Lead-gen deals bill per qualified lead but against a monthly minimum, and the client pays ad spend (KB `pricing-model`). "Only pay for results" reads as bait and switch the day the contract shows a minimum |
| Meta listed as a service | He runs Meta ads by hand today (his own car-detailer campaign). What is gated is the OS's API automation (KB `meta-marketing-api`), not his ability to manage an account |
| No button shapes, not even boxed checkmarks | His rule: nothing that looks tappable and is not. The real link is Instagram's **Link sticker**, added when posting |
| Dropped "I'll take a free look at your website" | He asked for something else. Replaced with the "tell you straight whether ads would pay off" line: easy to answer, and signals he might say no |
| A keyword to DM ("LEADS") | Removes the "what do I even say" friction, the main reason people who are interested do not message |
| Poll sticker | One tap is easier than a DM; he then messages every Yes, which is a hand-raised warm lead |
| Video first, graphic second | A real face beats any designed graphic for trust on a personal account |

## The video script given (read to camera, ~20 seconds)

> Hey, I'm Bryson. I run Google, Facebook and Instagram ads for service businesses. Remodelers,
> detailers, pool companies, cleaners.
> Here's what's different. You pay me per real lead. Not for clicks, and not for tire kickers.
> I'm only taking on a few. So if you want more booked jobs, DM me the word LEADS and I'll tell you
> straight whether ads would pay off for you.

No em dashes, no AI tells (KB `ad-copy-voice`). No client results claimed: there is one signed
client and no case study yet, so nothing may imply a track record.

## Still true when he does this again

- Post order: video, then the graphic with the poll sticker, then optionally a Link sticker to the site.
- 🔴 Do not pitch paid promotion of the story. No ad budget until he says the money is there (KB `niche-selection`).
