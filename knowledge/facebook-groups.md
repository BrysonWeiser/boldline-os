## Rules used (keep them for every group post)
- Read the group's rules first; many only allow promotion on one day of the week or in one thread.
- No link in the post (Facebook shows link posts to fewer people, and admins remove them). Put the link in a
  reply only when someone asks.
- Give before asking: a free, specific check, done by hand, with "no pitch attached", and keep that promise.
- Ask people to comment their business and area, so each comment opens a conversation he can move to DMs.
- Reply to every comment within the hour, then DM. Post Tuesday to Thursday morning.
- House copy rules: no em dashes, no "local businesses", no client counts or results that aren't true.

## The intro post (2026-10-12)
Hey everyone, I'm Bryson. I'm based here in Phoenix and I'm glad to be in the group.

I run BoldLine Media. I set up and run Google Ads for service businesses like HVAC, plumbing, handyman and
cleaning companies, and I build the landing page or website those ads send people to.

What I do differently: no big flat monthly fee whether the phone rings or not. Most of what I earn is tied to
real leads, meaning people who actually asked for a quote. Your ad budget goes straight to Google from your own
card, and the account stays yours. I'm also waiving the setup fee for my first few clients.

Not running ads and not looking to? I'll still take a free look at your website and your Google listing and send
you a short list of things that are probably costing you calls. No pitch attached.

Comment your business and the area you serve and I'll get to everyone this week. And if you just want to say hi,
tell me what you do. I'm always happy to send work to people in here.
