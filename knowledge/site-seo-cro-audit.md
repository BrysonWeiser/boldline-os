**IMPLEMENTED (marketing-site):**

- **Schema (head of index.html):** Organization gained `founder` (Bryson Weiser, E-E-A-T); new **Service** JSON-LD (Google/Meta ads mgmt + landing pages, areaServed AZ, $350 offer).

- **#2 Lead-Leak Check lead magnet:** a low-friction `<section id="lead-leak">` (website + email + honeypot) before the contact form; AJAX → **`marketing-site/netlify/functions/audit.mjs`** → inserts `website_leads` row `form:"lead_leak"` (reuses SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY; honeypot + email validation; fail-soft). Shows in the **OS Leads** screen (loadLeads only filters out `form:"newsletter"`). What a Lead-Leak Check is: a free mini-audit of a prospect's site/funnel/ads showing where they lose customers + the top fixes — a value-first hook that captures people not ready to book a call.
- **AUTOMATED (2026-08-07, "go full auto") — the bot delivers the audit itself.** After capturing the lead, `audit.mjs` fires a best-effort POST (with a shared secret) to a new OS background function **`netlify/functions/lead-leak-audit-background.mjs`**, which: (1) fetches the prospect's homepage and extracts on-page signals (title, meta desc, H1, viewport tag, tel: link, form, image/script counts, page weight, text sample), (2) runs `claude-opus-4-8` + `web_search` (pause_turn loop, same proven pattern as deal-research-background) for an honest 2-4 "leak" mini-audit under HARD accuracy rules (no fabricated stats/reviews/owner names; unconfirmed items phrased as questions; no emojis), (3) renders it into the branded DARK email shell (`emailShell` from client-emails-shared) with a gold "Book a Free 30-Minute Call" button → `calendly.com/theboldlinemedia/30min`, and (4) **emails it straight to the prospect and copies Bryson** ([Auto-sent to prospect] subject), then stamps `payload.auditedAt/auditStatus/auditSubject` on the lead so it's never double-sent (de-dupe/optimistic-claim guard; releases the claim on error for retry). Speed-to-lead: the prospect gets a real, specific audit in ~1 min instead of days later.
  - **Owner notifications (Bryson gets BOTH):** (a) an **instant "New Lead-Leak Check request" email** the moment someone submits — `audit.mjs` `notifyOwnerNewRequest` fires independently of the bot, so an unset secret / out-of-credits / unreachable-site all still notify him (dormant only if the marketing site lacks RESEND_API_KEY+REPORTS_FROM_EMAIL, which are set); plus the live new-lead toast/badge in the OS Leads tab; and (b) the **copy of the finished audit** (~1 min later, from the OS bot). Owner address = `process.env.OWNER_EMAIL` on marketing, else the hardcoded fallback (same as submission-created.mjs).
  - **Setup Bryson must do:** set **`AUDIT_TRIGGER_SECRET`** (any long random string, IDENTICAL value) on BOTH Netlify sites — marketing (`boldline-media`) to send it, OS (`boldlinemedia`) to verify. Until it's set the bot stays dormant (marketing skips the trigger; OS 401s) and the lead is captured + handled manually exactly as before. Also needs Anthropic credits (shared with Deal Prep — currently out). Optional kill-switch: **`LEAD_LEAK_REVIEW_ONLY=1`** on the OS site routes the audit to Bryson only (for review/forward) instead of auto-sending to the prospect. Optional `OS_ORIGIN` override on marketing (default `https://boldlinemedia.netlify.app`).
