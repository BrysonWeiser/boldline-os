## Assumptions used
Detailing lead fee $15 (pricing-shared niche fee); Meta Launch, ad budget from $500 on their card; setup $750 waived
on the founding offer; 3 months then month to month; Meta leads for detailing roughly $10 to $30 each, about 70%
qualified; average client budget ~$750 -> ~25 billable leads -> ~$350 a month to BoldLine. Founding first month of
lead fees free where offered.

## 12 months (founding scenario, 6 signers)
5 ads (mostly Meta, maybe 1 Google for ceramic), 1 website (build + care or outright), Complete 0 to 1 (a shop only),
Google profile care 0 to 1. Months 3 to 4: about 2 leave (lead quality, slow replies, "Instagram is free"). Summer
(June to August): 1 pauses or leaves (heat, slow season) and success churn (a solo guy who fills up pauses ads).
Month 12: about 3 still paying. Revenue ~ $14k (range $9k to $20k). Full price: ~3 signers, ~ $9k including setups.
To earn $10k a month from detailers alone needs roughly 30 active detailer clients.
