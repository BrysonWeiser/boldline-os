## The Springbok example used
Referral or search -> they look Dr. Gibb up on their phone -> his site has to answer "is he legit, does he treat my
problem, does he take my insurance, how do I book" in seconds. Services worth a page each: TMJ (what he wants to
market), back pain, sciatica, headaches, sports injuries, in Gilbert/Mesa/Chandler. Insurance answer (BCBS etc.) and
online booking via ChiroTouch. Example math, labelled as an example: if a new patient is worth ~$1,000 over their
care and a better site wins 2 more a month, that's ~$24,000 a year against a $1,500 build. Get his real number.
Note: Bryson also works for Springbok, so any website pitch to Dr. Gibb carries the conflict disclosure
(KB springbok-wellness-deal).

## Spotting a weak site in 30 seconds (on a phone)
Slow to load; no call or book button without scrolling; no reviews; one page for everything; no insurance or prices
answer; old year in the footer; no padlock; looks like every other template in the trade.
