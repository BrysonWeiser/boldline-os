## Notes
- Name must be the real business name ("MB Auto Experts"). Adding "Mobile Detailing Phoenix" to the name breaks
  Google's rules and can get the profile suspended.
- Service area: up to 20 cities or ZIP codes, roughly within two hours' drive of base.
- Verification method varies (video, phone, text, email, sometimes mail); Google decides. It can take a few days.
- This is the free setup. BoldLine's paid Google profile care ($130/mo, KB google-listing) is the ongoing part:
  every review answered and a post every week.
