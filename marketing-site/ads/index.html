The journey of one lead

From a search to your phone, and into your report.

  1. They search.Someone types what they need into Google, ready to book.
  2. They see your ad.Your ad sits at the top, written for exactly that search.
  3. They land on a page made for it.Not your homepage. A page about the job they searched for, with your reviews and one clear next step.
  4. They ask for a quote.A short form or a tap to call. It takes them under a minute.
  5. Your phone buzzes.The lead reaches you right away, tagged with the ad that brought it in.
  6. You see what it was worth.Each month, a plain report: what you spent, what came in, and what we're changing next.
