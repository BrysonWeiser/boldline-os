Ads
How much does this cost?
There's no monthly fee for ads. You pay a set fee for each qualified lead we deliver, agreed on the call and written into your agreement, so a month with no leads costs you nothing. Online stores pay 15% of their ad spend instead (12% at the biggest budgets), with no minimum, and there's a $300 a month Complete plan for stores too. If you want your website, Google profile and the rest handled too, the Complete plan is $500 a month plus the lead fee, with the website built free. Your ad budget is on top, usually $1,000 a month or more, and goes straight to Google or Meta. See the pricing page.
Do I own my ad account?
Always. Your Google and Meta accounts stay in your name and are billed directly to you. We only ever hold manager-level access to run them, and we never hold, front, or touch your ad spend.
Why is there a three month minimum to start?
Because paid ads compound. The first month is mostly learning which keywords and audiences actually convert, the second puts that data to work, and by the third the system has real momentum you can judge fairly. Anything shorter sets both of us up to quit right before the results show. After those first three months we earn it month to month, and either of us can stop with 30 days' notice. Your budget never scales without your sign-off.
What counts as a qualified lead?
A real person in your area asking about work you do, through a call or a form that came from your ads. Spam, salespeople and duplicates never count. We agree the details with you before you pay for a single one, and you can see every lead you're billed for.
What's in the Complete plan?
Your ads, run exactly like the Ads plan, plus a five page website built free, hosting and care, your Google profile kept busy, a blog, emails that bring past customers back, and online booking with deposits. It's $500 a month plus your lead fee. It starts with 12 months, which is what pays for the free website, then goes month to month. See everything that's included.
How fast will I see results?
The first few weeks are mostly learning which keywords and audiences convert. Paid ads compound over time; they're not a light switch. And if your offer or follow-up isn't ready to make the most of the clicks, we'll tell you before you spend, not after. More on that here.
What if it's not working?
We review every account on a set schedule against real performance data and report in plain English, so you always know what's spending, what's converting, and what's wasted. If something isn't working, we change it. If we're genuinely not the right fit, we'll say so.
Which platforms do you run?
Google Ads and Meta, on their own or as one system, plus the landing pages, call tracking, and lead routing behind them. See the packages.
Websites
Who owns the website and the domain?
You do. Your domain stays registered in your name the whole time. Once the build is paid, the words on your site are yours too, along with your logo and photos. If you ever stop the care plan, we'll send you a copy of your site's pages and images so you can host it anywhere you like.
How long does a website take?
You'll see a first version within about two weeks of your first payment and the details we need from you. You get two rounds of changes before anything goes live, and it only goes live once you've approved it.
Do you write the words, or do I?
We do. We write every page from what you tell us about your business, and you read all of it before it goes live. Send us photos of your own work if you have them. Until then we use licensed stock photos, and we never pass them off as your work.
Can I make changes after it's live?
Yes, just tell us what to change. The care plan includes up to two small changes a month, like text, photos, hours or prices. Bigger changes get a quote first, so there are no surprises.
What happens if I stop the care plan?
Give us 30 days' notice and you're free to go. Your domain is already yours. We'll send you a copy of your site to host wherever you want, and tell you exactly which domain settings we made so whoever's next can pick it up.
